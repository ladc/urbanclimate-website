We were once again at the I Love Science Festival, held in Brussels from 10 to 12 October 2025. This edition drew around 17,000 participants, and our WATS ambassador Lesley De Cruz had the honour of being the festival's "godmother" this year.

As always, our LEGO temperature boxes were a big hit. Many children designed their own climate-resilient city in LEGO and immediately saw the impact of their choices on the local temperature, learning in a playful way about the link between land use and urban heat.

It was wonderful to see so many curious kids and parents building, testing and discovering together. Thank you to everyone who stopped by our stand, and to Innoviris and visit.Brussels for organizing another fantastic festival!
